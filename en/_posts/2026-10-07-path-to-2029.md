Every dog we place begins as a promise — 
to a veteran learning to trust the world again, 
to a child facing anxiety no one else can see, 
to someone stepping into public life without fear. 
Keeping that promise at scale takes a plan.
<!--excerpt-end-->

Over the next four years, we're expanding every placement track responsibly. 
Emotional Support Dogs grow from 45 to 60 placements. 
PTSD Service Dogs nearly double, from 25 to 50, meeting urgent need among veterans and trauma survivors. 
Guide Dogs, our founding program, grow steadily from 32 to 41.  

To make all this possible, we are expanding our breeding program and gaining expertise in the use of frozen sperm.

Together, total annual partnerships rise from 113 in 2026 to 166 in 2029 — a 47% increase in lives changed.

We are growing, with your support behind us. 
Every gift brings a dog closer to graduation and a person closer to independence. 
Help us turn these targets into 166 real partnerships a year.
