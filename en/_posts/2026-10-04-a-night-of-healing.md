On May 11, 2026, the Israel Guide Dog Center hosted a major donor event at a longtime supporter's home in Savyon to raise vital awareness for our PTSD Service Dog Program. 
As of August 2026, this stood as our first large-scale event in nearly six years. 
The moving evening brought together approximately 300 guests.
<!--excerpt-end-->

The Master of Ceremonies was Israeli actor Imri Biton, an early graduate of our PTSD program, who attended alongside his service dog, Chance. 
Following an opening address by Noach Braun, 
there was a deeply moving segment featured a graduate named Adam, 
who shared his personal journey and the life-changing impact of his PTSD service dog, Silver. 
Guests were also treated to incredible performances by sand-artist Ilana Yahav and renowned singer-songwriter Idan Raichel, 
who brought his iconic fusion of electronic and traditional sounds to the stage.
