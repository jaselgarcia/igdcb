To address the shortage of qualified guide dog trainers in Israel, the Israel Guide Dog Center (IGDCB) has sent two apprentice trainers to Vision Australia/Seeing Guide Dogs. 
This followed a highly successful pilot program from 2022 to 2023, aligned with the International Guide Dog Federation standards.
<!--excerpt-end-->

At the beginning of 2026, we hired two talented new apprentice trainers: Ido Sonego and Noga Elbaz. 
They are currently in Melbourne completing their first-year apprenticeship. 
They will return home in April 2027 to finish their training at the Center, to be certified as Guide Dog Trainers and to continue their studies and apprenticeship to become Guide Dog Mobility Instructors for people with blindness and visual impairment.

Ido and Noga are being mentored by Yoav Artov, a senior trainer in Vision Australia.  
Yoav worked for the Israel Guide Dog Center, prior to moving to Australia.  
His knowledge of Israeli culture and our standards is a huge advantage.
