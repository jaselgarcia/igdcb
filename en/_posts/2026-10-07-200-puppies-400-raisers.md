Did you know our department currently supports over 200 puppies with the help of nearly 400 dedicated raisers across Israel? 
Most of these amazing volunteers are university students!
One of our biggest goals right now is keeping our experienced puppy raisers on board. 
Seasoned families bring valuable practical knowledge, 
providing a stable, low-stress environment that helps puppies thrive in their training as guide, PTSD, or emotional support dogs. 
Plus, it allows our staff to fully focus on mentoring newcomers.
<!--excerpt-end-->

To set our new families up for success, 
we have completely refreshed our training by launching new materials, 
setting up a daily messaging support system, and introducing seasoned raisers as mentors. 
We also continue to celebrate our community with two major annual events: 
an inspiring meeting with our program beneficiaries and a fun-filled Purim celebration. 
Thank you for making our mission possible!
