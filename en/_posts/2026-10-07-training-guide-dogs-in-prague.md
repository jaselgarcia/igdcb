The demand for guide, service, and emotional support dogs in Israel significantly outpaces our local training capacity. 
To meet this critical need, 
the Israel Guide Dog Center launched an exciting strategic partnership in 2025 with the Czech Guide Dog School, a part of Czech Blind United (SONS). 
This collaboration is a perfect match: they have experienced trainers, and we have dogs that need to be trained. 
The Czech trainers are teaching our dogs in Hebrew, ensuring they meet our strict Israeli standards. 
Our two newest international trainees, Sheldon and Phoenix, arrived in Prague at the end of April and are settling beautifully into the initial stages of their training. 
We look forward to sharing their progress as they prepare to change lives.
