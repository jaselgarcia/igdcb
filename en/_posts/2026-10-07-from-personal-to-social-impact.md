A guide dog or service dog first and foremost changes an individual's life: 
it enables independence, mobility, safety, and the ability to act without depending on others. 
However, the impact does not end there. 
A more independent and mobile person can study, work, create, consume, and actively participate in society. 
Therefore, the value of a service dog is measured not only by the change in the life of the person who received it, 
but also by the value it returns to society. 
When we invest in a person, we are not just changing a life—we are amplifying society as a whole.
